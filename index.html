How to export a chat from WhatsApp
  1. Open the chat or group in WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Android: tap the three dots, then More, then Export chat. iPhone: tap the chat name at the top, scroll down, then Export Chat.
  3. Choose Without media. The export is smaller and the analysis is the same.
  4. Send the file to yourself (email, Drive, Files) and open this page on the device where you saved it.

Works with Android and iPhone exports, 12 or 24 hour clocks. English and Spanish are fully supported; Portuguese, German, French and Italian on a best-effort basis. Reply times count only replies sent within 12 hours. A new conversation starts after 8 hours of silence.
